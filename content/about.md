Faith Leader Hub is a free, practical resource for the people who keep churches of every size running: pastors, church administrators, office staff and the volunteers who quietly hold everything together.

We write about the everyday work of ministry. That means planning the week in the church office, welcoming guests, scheduling volunteers, keeping records accurate and safe, and choosing the tools that fit your congregation. Our aim is simple, honest advice you can put to use this week, without hype or jargon.

Faith Leader Hub is published by Pentesoft, the team behind [SteepleMate](https://get.steeplemate.com), church management software built for congregations like yours. We mention SteepleMate where it's genuinely relevant, but these articles are written to help you whatever tools you use.

All articles are free to read. You're welcome to share them with your staff, your volunteers or other church leaders, and to reprint them in your newsletter, as long as you credit Faith Leader Hub and link back to the original.

If you have a question you'd like us to write about, we'd be glad to hear it.
